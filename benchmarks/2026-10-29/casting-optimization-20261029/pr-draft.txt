Title: perf: reduce repeated document-fusion overhead during casting

Casting repeatedly fuses ordinary dictionaries and VertexRep observations. The existing implementation constructs four nested helper functions on each fusion group and routes ordinary dictionaries through the general representation checks. This change moves the helpers to module scope and specializes ordinary lists of dictionaries. Empty fragments are queued until merging, so callbacks from later keys can still populate them.

The general path remains for subclasses and VertexRep inputs. Regression coverage checks identity hashing, keyless fragments, union behaviour, shallow references, ordering, invalid inputs, callback-driven mutations and errors. Two properties compare plain and general fusion paths over generated inputs. An opt-in casting benchmark checks values outside its measured interval and adds no timing threshold to CI.

Performance remains conditional:

- Four fresh-server file-ETL pairs show 2.46% median wall-time reduction; this is exploratory, not a precise or general speedup claim.
